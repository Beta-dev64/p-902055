# Reference evaluations

## Google Antigravity — https://antigravity.google/

The strongest transferable ideas are a very quiet white canvas, a compact navigation bar with a single dark download action, an oversized center-set proposition, product storytelling through actual interface imagery, and a clear progression from promise to product surfaces to use cases to editorial content. Its hero uses a small brand marker above the headline, generous negative space, and simple dual CTAs. FuseLabs should borrow the hierarchy and product-proof discipline, not the Google branding or exact copy.

## Lama Lama — https://lamalama.com/

The strongest transferable ideas are the agency voice, a menu-driven navigation that expands into a full-screen visual system, an oversized pitch-deck/CTA motif, case studies treated as interactive stories rather than ordinary cards, mixed media including video, and repeated kinetic typography that makes calls to action feel alive. Its project archive is particularly useful: each case has a clear category signal, a narrative description, and a deliberate transition into deeper work. FuseLabs should translate that into technically truthful case-study evidence and preserve its darker, more engineered identity.

## Direction so far

The redesign should combine Antigravity’s calm product clarity with Lama Lama’s expressive agency pacing. Space Grotesk becomes the primary typeface, with a restrained display hierarchy, expanded full-width wordmark moments, interactive project storytelling, and motion that is concentrated around navigation, hero proof, case-study transitions, and final conversion rather than applied uniformly.

## The Web Addicts — https://thewebaddicts.com/

The strongest transferable ideas are a numbered, almost editorial navigation system; a dark hero with a visual object offset against a large text block; a high-volume work archive with category labels and short context; a strong transition into a direct contact statement; and a footer that makes contact information, social links, and legal utility feel like part of the same composition. The site also demonstrates that a substantial work archive can be made scannable through repeated structure without flattening it into a generic card grid. FuseLabs should adapt the sequence and evidence density while keeping its own technical/product voice.

## Combined reference thesis

The new FuseLabs system will be called **Signal in Motion**: a technical agency identity where every major interaction makes a delivery signal easier to read. Antigravity contributes calm clarity and product evidence; Lama Lama contributes expressive agency energy and case-study theatre; The Web Addicts contributes numbered navigation, archive density, and direct contact composition. The result should be a Space Grotesk-led system with charcoal, bone, amber, and academy cobalt roles, stronger full-bleed transitions, and a single owned motion vocabulary shared across agency and academy.

## Verification notes

Desktop and compact mobile captures show the new Space Grotesk system loading across home, portfolio, and academy. The home now reads as a long-form signal narrative with the amber capability ticker between partner proof and services. The portfolio archive is now a numbered work index with a stronger case-study hierarchy and mobile recomposition. The academy retains its separate paper/cobalt identity and cohort-first CTA.
